    Game modes

    Classic

    Every team is equal. Pure chaos: any club can beat any other, it all comes down to bounces, the keeper's form and a bit of luck.

    Realistic

    Team strength comes from real-world Elo ratings, updated daily. Stronger teams finish better and win more duels, so favourites win more often, tuned to match real-world odds. Upsets still happen.

    Ratings: clubelo.com (or footballdatabase.com when it is unavailable) for clubs, eloratings.net for national teams.

    The mode is locked while a match is being played and for the whole of a tournament.
